## Straightforward IT Ops and Cybersecurity

CSInc helps businesses and organizations track down IT problems, identify where information is exposed, and decide what to fix first.

- **Security reviews.** We can help you audit your website, mobile app, APIs, cloud configuration, etc. Findings explain what was accessible, how we verified the exposure, and what needs to change.
- **Wi-Fi and networks.** Wi-Fi works at reception but drops out upstairs? CSInc can check coverage, interference, and capacity before you spend money on a bunch of new access points.
- **Systems and infrastructure.** We can help you troubleshoot Linux systems, review a network design, or give you a second opinion on a software change, including AI integration and all the buzzwords.

What’s giving you trouble? Happy to take a look.
